Where tourism fits

Tourism contributes 7.77% of total GSVA (about one-sixth of services). It runs through trade, hotels & restaurants, transport, and other services. See the Tourism tab.

Industry (secondary sector): 39.4% of the economy

Share of the industry sector. In the Survey’s table, mining & quarrying (₹1,002 cr) is counted under the primary sector, not here.

Where industry sits

  • Solan district’s Baddi-Barotiwala-Nalagarh (BBN) belt holds over 90% of the state’s industry, per a Himtimes report citing the Economic Survey 2024-25. It accounts for about 29% of state GST and 45% of its electricity consumption.
  • Solan’s per capita income is about ₹8.1 lakh, nearly three times the state average.
  • See the Pharma and Hydropower tabs for the two biggest manufacturing and utility stories.

Agriculture & allied (primary sector): 14.3% of the economy

Share of the primary sector. Forestry is the second-largest piece, which surprises many people. Apples and other fruit sit inside “Crops”.

  • Agriculture & allied GSVA at current prices rose from ₹22,428 cr (2021-22) to ₹32,415 cr (2025-26). Crops went from ₹13,722 cr to ₹18,515 cr.
  • About 54% of workers are in the primary sector, so farm incomes matter far more than the 14% output share suggests.
  • The Apples tab covers the state’s main cash crop in detail.

Apple crop by season (crore boxes)

Apple is a biennial-bearing crop with big swings. The 2026 season looks poor: the Director of Horticulture estimated about 4.36 lakh t (−40%) in a Tribune report, and a later report says market arrivals had reached only 1.29 lakh t by 14 Oct 2026 against 4.20 lakh t by 14 Sep last year. Debt and fiscal health

Total debt per budget documents (₹ crore)

From the “budget in brief”, as reported by HimachalScape. 2025-26 and 2026-27 are projections. Outstanding liabilities are 41% of GSDP in 2025-26 (revised) and 40.5% in 2026-27 (budget), per PRS.

Why you’ll see different debt numbers

Figures differ by date and by definition: loans only, or total liabilities including provident funds and other public-account items. Treat them as ranges, not contradictions.

Dec 2022 (CM’s figure)₹76,633 cr

The CM also cites ~₹11,000 cr of other liabilities (DA arrears and pay-commission dues). Debt is a politically contested number, so check the original statements.

Other stress indicators

  • Fiscal deficit hit 6.6% of GSDP in 2025-26 (revised) against 4% budgeted. The 16th Finance Commission limit is 3%.
  • Outstanding government guarantees were ₹3,100 cr (1.34% of GSDP) in March 2025.
  • Revenue arrears stood at ₹6,289 cr in March 2024, half of them over five years old (CAG).
  • Power subsidy rose from ₹579 cr (2018-19) to ₹1,936 cr (2024-25) per the 16th Finance Commission. Its study found 69.7% of top-quintile households got free electricity.

Private companies’ revenue and share

I could not find a reliable public source that ranks private HP companies by revenue or share of state output. Unlisted private firms file financials with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, so the figures are available per company there for a fee, but there is no state-level compilation. I have not estimated numbers I can’t source. For public-sector weight, SJVN is the main state-linked listed firm (Government of India 59.92%, HP Government 26.85%, per Wikipedia).
